AI agents / reliability harness

Equity Research Agent Harness

End-to-end LangGraph research-agent harness that turns a US stock ticker into an auditable equity research memo, with typed data collection, evidence packaging, grounding verification, compliance checks, and local traces designed for replay and evaluation.

  • Built an explicit graph workflow from ticker validation through data collection, thesis construction, memo drafting, grounding, compliance, and finalization.
  • Implemented typed tools for company profile, market data, SEC filing facts, and news or soft-signal collection.
  • Separated human-readable memo output from structured intermediate state so evaluators can inspect agent behavior without parsing only Markdown.
  • Kept the MVP non-personalized and deterministic before adding live LLM calls, making reliability tests repeatable.

Polymarket Weather Market Intelligence & Live Trading System

End-to-end same-day weather-market system that collects official weather and market inputs, builds canonical prediction snapshots, serves inference, applies online correction, and runs live/smoke trading flows with risk controls and settlement checks.

  • Built FastAPI runtime, scheduler ingestion, PostgreSQL storage, and Docker services.
